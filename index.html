Before your visit

A few helpful details to make your appointment feel smooth and stress-free.

Please arrive 5–10 minutes early so you have time to check in, ask questions, and settle in before your session begins.

If you have a recent injury, ongoing pain, or a medical condition, please consult your healthcare provider first and let us know before your session.

Please call as soon as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule, so the appointment time can be made available for another guest.

Parking is available near the location at 3845 Bonney Rd #108 in Virginia Beach. Please allow a few extra minutes during busy hours.
